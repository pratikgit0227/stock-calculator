📥
Enter your Buy transactions
Add each purchase — date, quantity, and buy price. Use + Add Row for multiple buys, or paste directly from Excel using the 📋 button. Supports DD/MM/YYYY format.
📤
Add Sell / loss-booking entries (optional)
If you've already sold some shares or booked a partial loss, enter those here. The calculator will recompute your breakeven on the remaining shares.
🎯
Set your Exit Strategy (optional)
Choose a holding period and a return benchmark — Bank FD, Nifty 50 / Sensex, or your own target %. The calculator will show your target sell price, expected gain, and tax (STCG/LTCG).
📊
Hit Calculate — get your full picture
See your new breakeven price, holding status, target exit price, tax liability, and a decision card: should you sell now or wait for LTCG?
💡 Tab 2 — STCG → LTCG Tracker: Enter buy lots with dates — see which are STCG vs LTCG, unrealised P&L, and how much tax you save by waiting.
📈 Tab 3 — Stock Tracker: Search NSE/BSE stocks, track daily OHLC & corporate events, get WhatsApp closing price alerts at 3:30 PM IST — free, no login.
